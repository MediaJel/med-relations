## Hotfixes

### Silent errors in advertiser funnel job + Fix for large advertisers

An advertiser funnel silently stopped updating because the job queue reported success even when individual jobs failed, masking a gateway timeout on large advertisers and a database transaction timeout during the final write step. The fix makes failures surface properly, batches and retries the impressions calls for large advertisers, and raises the transaction limits so large report writes can complete.

### Include paused campaigns in get-events and consolidate processing

Paused campaigns were being skipped entirely by the event-fetching and report-consolidation jobs (and their newer counterparts), so their data was never picked up while paused. This hotfix adds the paused statuses to the existing filters so paused, in-flight campaigns are processed the same way live campaigns are.
